Live Desk — paper orders on real Kalshi open contracts

The desk places paper orders against OPEN event contracts priced ONLY from data this repository captured from the official Kalshi API: the real bid ladder (GET /markets/{ticker}/orderbook), the real quoted touch, the contract's own close_time / expiration_time, the real traded volume and open interest, the official quadratic fee with the captured series multiplier, and the exchange's own settlement. An order is never filled at a price no real resting order was showing, and a ladder captured after the order time is refused rather than used.

Where these prices come from: every ladder on this desk carries the timestamp it was captured and the exact URL it came from. A ladder is a snapshot, not a live stream: the desk is a paper simulator that re-prices itself every time the ingest job captures a new book, and it labels each contract that has no captured ladder as not tradeable here instead of inventing depth.

Desk Season — one book carried across every real capture round

The Live Desk runs one session at one cut-off. This section runs the carried book: each round is a real capture batch from the store, and cash, positions and resting orders carry from round to round. Between rounds the book is walked through the real events in that window — later captured ladders, later captured candlesticks and the exchange’s own settlements.

What makes this a forward test, not a backtest: every round is a moment this repository actually queried the exchange’s order books, and the engine refuses to price an order with a ladder captured after the order’s own timestamp. A strategy sees only the point-in-time view — quotes, ladders, bars and positions available at its round. Settlements are the exchange’s own result and settlement_value_dollars.

Verified Trade Ledger

Every fill and every round trip this engine has booked, with the real market period it traded in (the candlestick's own end_period_ts — never the wall clock of a run), the executed price, the slippage paid into the real quoted touch, which order-book ladder priced it, the share of the bar's real traded volume it took, the official fee, and the exchange's own settlement where one exists. The ledger is re-derived from the engine on every export and refused if a single field disagrees (scripts/export-ledger.mjs → data/ledger/).
