Mathematical Proof: Kalshi Reciprocal Order Book Mechanics

In a binary prediction market, every event contract has two outcomes: YES and NO. A winning contract settles at $1.00 (100¢), and a losing contract settles at $0.00. Because holding 1 YES + 1 NO guarantees a payout of exactly $1.00:

P(YES) + P(NO) = $1.00 (100¢)

Kalshi's API orderbook publishes bids only (wrapped in orderbook_fp with yes_dollars and no_dollars). Asks are derived reciprocally:

Implied YES Ask = $1.00 - Best NO Bid Implied NO Ask = $1.00 - Best YES Bid YES Spread = Implied YES Ask - Best YES Bid

Our paper simulation engine implements this reciprocal model identically, ensuring that market orders consume actual liquidity depth and experience volume-weighted slippage.
